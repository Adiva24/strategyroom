The Idea

About StrategyRoom

Most business education hands you a conclusion. A case study tells you what Company X did, what happened, and what to learn from it — and by the time you're reading it, the lesson has already been decided for you.

StrategyRoom works the other way. You walk into a situation before it's resolved. You read the same data a real decision-maker would have had, weigh constraints that don't fully cooperate with each other, and choose — knowing that whatever you pick is final, the way real decisions are.

Why decisions, not lectures

Judgment isn't built by being told the right answer. It's built by making a choice, living with its consequence, and noticing — clearly, specifically — why it played out the way it did. That noticing is where the actual learning happens.

What's inside

One hundred scenarios across branding, marketing, entrepreneurship, customer psychology, leadership, and ethical business — each inspired by the kinds of situations real companies have faced, reimagined as original simulations rather than reporting on any specific company's internal affairs.

A note on accuracy

Every scenario here is an original simulation built for strategic reasoning practice. None claims to represent verified internal information from any real company — they're built to be realistic, not factual.
